Clawd

8 looping actions, rendered headless in Blender and packed into CSS sprite strips. No JavaScript.

160px cells at 24fps, driven by steps() on background-position — one image request per action, no decoder, no script. Cells are opaque: the renders contain both a sky and a floor region, so no single page colour hides their edges, and they are framed as tiles instead. Transparent sprites would need a second render pass with film_transparent, which in EEVEE also means losing the ground shadow — shadow catchers are Cycles-only.
